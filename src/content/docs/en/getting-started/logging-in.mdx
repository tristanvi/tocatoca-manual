---
title: Logging In
description: How to sign in to TocaToca.
---

TocaToca accounts are managed through [auth.tocatoca.com](https://auth.tocatoca.com). After a successful login, you will be automatically redirected to the TocaToca player at [play.tocatoca.com](https://play.tocatoca.com).

## Sign In

Open the login page and enter the email address and password associated with your TocaToca account.

![Login](/images/manual/login-001.jpg)

## After Logging In

Once you have successfully signed in, you will be redirected to the TocaToca player.

From there, you can browse the catalog, discover recordings, and start listening immediately.

## If You Cannot Log In

Please check the following:

* that the email address has been entered correctly
* that the password has been entered correctly
* whether Caps Lock is enabled
* that you are using the correct account

If you have forgotten your password, you can request a password reset from the login page.

For additional help, please see [Login Problems](/en/account-access/login-problems/).
