description: A brief introduction to the world of historical recordings.

TocaToca is a streaming service dedicated to historical recordings.

The initial catalog is based on TangoTunes and features carefully restored transfers from the shellac era. Additional collections and musical genres will be added over time.

TocaToca was created for people who want to discover, explore, and enjoy historical music. The focus is on providing simple access to recordings — directly in the browser and without technical barriers.

![Home Page](/images/manual/home-001.jpg)

## Discover Music

With TocaToca, you can:

* explore artists and orchestras
* discover historical recordings
* browse collections
* save favorites
* create your own playlists
* explore connections between artists, orchestras, and recordings

Many elements throughout the catalog are clickable and lead directly to related recordings.

## Streaming Instead of Downloading

TocaToca is designed for listening directly in your browser.

Recordings are streamed and do not need to be downloaded to your device. This gives you immediate access to the current catalog and allows you to start listening right away.

## Who Is TocaToca For?

TocaToca is designed for:

* music lovers
* collectors
* dancers
* tango enthusiasts
* clubs and dance schools
* anyone interested in discovering historical recordings

Whether you are searching for a specific recording or simply browsing and exploring, TocaToca makes access to the music of the shellac era as simple as possible.
