---
title: Activate Your Subscription
description: How to unlock access to the TocaToca catalog.
---

An active subscription unlocks access to the available music catalog.

## Check Your Subscription Status

Sign in at [auth.tocatoca.com](https://auth.tocatoca.com) and review the status of your subscription.

![Subscription Status](/images/manual/abonnementstatus.jpg)

## After Activation

Open [play.tocatoca.com](https://play.tocatoca.com).

Once your subscription is active, you can browse the catalog, discover recordings, and start listening immediately.

Alternatively, you can use the **“Open Player”** button to go directly to the TocaToca player.

## If Access Is Not Activated

Reload the page and, if necessary, sign out and sign in again.

If your subscription still does not appear as active, please refer to [Subscription Status](/en/account-access/subscription-status/) for additional guidance.
