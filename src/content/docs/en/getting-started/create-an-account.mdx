A personal account is required to use TocaToca. Guest access is currently not available.

## Registration

Open [auth.tocatoca.com](https://auth.tocatoca.com) and follow the steps to create your account.

![Create Account](/images/manual/de-konto-erstellen01.jpg)

![Create Account](/images/manual/de-konto-erstellen02.jpg)

## Choose the Right Subscription

Two subscription options are available for personal use:

* €10 per month
* €100 per year

If you wish to use TocaToca for a dance school, club, association, or similar organization, an organizer subscription is available for €200 per year.

Choose the subscription that best fits your needs.

![Choose Subscription](/images/manual/abonnement.jpg)

### Note for Professional DJs

TocaToca is intended for personal listening as well as use by dance schools, clubs, and event organizers.

Professional tango DJs who perform at paid events require separate usage rights. These rights are not included in the standard subscription.

## Payment via Stripe

After selecting your subscription, you will be automatically redirected to Stripe. Stripe handles secure payment processing for TocaToca.

Please enter the required information, including your payment details.

![Stripe](/images/manual/stripe.jpg)

If you cancel your subscription within three days of signing up, no charges will be applied.

## Activation

Once registration and payment have been completed successfully, your account will be activated automatically.

You can then log in to TocaToca and start listening immediately.

## Next Steps

You may wish to check your [Subscription Status](/en/account-access/subscription-status/) or read the guidelines on [Responsible Use](/en/account-access/responsible-use/) of your account.
