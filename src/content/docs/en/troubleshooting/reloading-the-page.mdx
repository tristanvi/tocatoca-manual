---
title: Reloading the Page
description: A simple step that can solve many common issues.
---

Many minor problems can be resolved simply by reloading the page.

Examples include:

* playback that does not start
* partially loaded content
* temporary connection issues
* an interface that is not responding correctly

## Reload the Page

Use your browser's reload button or the appropriate keyboard shortcut.

This refreshes the current page and establishes a new connection to TocaToca.

## Start Playback Again

After reloading the page, try playing the desired recording again.

If you have been logged out in the meantime, sign in again and reopen the player.

## Restart Your Browser

If reloading does not help, completely close your browser and open it again.

Then sign in to TocaToca once more.

## When to Contact Support

If the problem continues after several attempts, please contact <a href="mailto:support@tocatoca.com">[support@tocatoca.com](mailto:support@tocatoca.com)</a>.

The following information will help us investigate the issue more quickly:

* what you were trying to do
* any error message that was displayed
* the device and browser you are using

The more information you can provide, the easier it is for us to identify the cause of the problem.
