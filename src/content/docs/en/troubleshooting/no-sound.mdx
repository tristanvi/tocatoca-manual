---
title: No Sound
description: First steps to take when playback is running but no sound can be heard.
---

If playback is running but no sound is audible, most issues can be resolved with a few simple checks.

## Check the Volume

Make sure the volume is turned up:

* in the TocaToca player
* in your browser
* on your computer, tablet, or smartphone

![No Sound](/images/manual/no-sound-volume.jpg)

## Try Another Recording

Start a different recording.

This helps determine whether the problem affects only one recording or playback in general.

## Reload the Page

Reload the page and start playback again.

## Check Your Audio Output

Make sure your device is using the correct audio output.

Especially on computers, audio may be routed to a different device, such as:

* Bluetooth headphones
* external speakers
* AirPlay devices
* HDMI monitors or televisions

If necessary, select the correct audio output in your device’s sound settings.

## If You Still Cannot Hear Anything

Sign out and sign in again, or restart your browser completely.

If the problem persists, please contact <a href="mailto:support@tocatoca.com">[support@tocatoca.com](mailto:support@tocatoca.com)</a>.
