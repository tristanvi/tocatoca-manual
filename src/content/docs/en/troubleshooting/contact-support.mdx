If you cannot solve a problem yourself, please contact the <a href="mailto:support@tocatoca.com">TocaToca Support Team</a>.

The more detailed your description, the faster we can help.

## Helpful Information

Please describe as clearly as possible:

* what you were trying to do
* what happened instead
* whether an error message was displayed
* whether the problem occurs regularly or only occasionally

## Please Include

The following information is particularly helpful when troubleshooting:

* the email address associated with your TocaToca account
* the device you are using (computer, smartphone, or tablet)
* the browser you are using
* the recording, collection, or page where the problem occurs
* a screenshot of the error message, if available

## Do Not Send Sensitive Information

Please never send:

* your password
* credit card information
* other confidential account credentials

The TocaToca Support Team will never ask you for your password.
