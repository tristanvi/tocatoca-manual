---
title: Playback Does Not Start
description: Help when a recording does not start playing.
---

If a recording does not start, the cause is usually related to your login session, internet connection, or a temporary browser issue.

## Reload the Page

Reload [play.tocatoca.com](https://play.tocatoca.com) and try starting playback again.

In many cases, simply reloading the page restores the connection to the streaming service.

## Check Your Login

If your session has expired, TocaToca may no longer be able to play recordings.

In this case, sign in again via [auth.tocatoca.com](https://auth.tocatoca.com) and try playback once more.

## Check Your Internet Connection

Verify that other websites are loading correctly.

With a slow or unstable connection, it may take a few seconds before a recording begins to play.

Without an active internet connection, streaming is not possible.

## Try Another Recording

Try playing a different recording.

This helps determine whether the issue affects only a specific recording or playback in general.

## Restart Your Browser

Close your browser completely and open it again.

Then sign in once more and test playback.

## Still Having Problems?

If recordings still do not start, please contact <a href="mailto:support@tocatoca.com">[support@tocatoca.com](mailto:support@tocatoca.com)</a>.

The following information will help us investigate the issue more quickly:

* the device you are using
* browser name and version
* when the problem occurred
* the affected recording or collection

This information helps us identify the cause more efficiently.
