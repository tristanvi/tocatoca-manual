---
title: Login Is Not Remembered
description: What to do if you have to sign in repeatedly.
---

Normally, TocaToca remembers your login on a device. If you have to sign in again every time you visit, a browser setting is usually preventing your session from being stored.

## Allow Cookies

TocaToca uses cookies to keep you signed in between visits.

Make sure that cookies and website data are not being blocked for TocaToca.

## Avoid Private Browsing Mode

In private or incognito mode, login sessions are often deleted automatically when the browser window is closed.

For regular use, we recommend using a normal browser window.

## Restart Your Browser

Close your browser completely and open it again.

Then sign in to TocaToca once more.

## Check Browser Extensions

Some privacy, ad-blocking, or security extensions can prevent login information from being stored.

If you use such extensions, try temporarily disabling them and test the login again.

## Sign In Again

Log in again through [auth.tocatoca.com](https://auth.tocatoca.com).

If the login is stored successfully, you should be recognized automatically during future visits.

## Still Having Problems?

If your login still is not being remembered, please contact <a href="mailto:support@tocatoca.com">[support@tocatoca.com](mailto:support@tocatoca.com)</a>.

Please include:

* the device you are using
* the browser you are using
* whether the problem occurs constantly or only occasionally

This information helps us identify the cause more quickly.
