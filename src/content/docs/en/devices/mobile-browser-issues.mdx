---
title: Mobile Browser Issues
description: Help with playback and login on smartphones and tablets.
---

Mobile browsers behave differently from desktop browsers in certain situations. This can affect login sessions, audio playback, and how browser data is stored.

## My Login Is Not Remembered

Modern mobile devices often use strict privacy and cookie settings.

As a result, you may occasionally be asked to sign in again, even if you have used TocaToca before.

## Sign In Again

Open [auth.tocatoca.com](https://auth.tocatoca.com) and sign in again using your email address and password.

After a successful login, click **"Open Player"** to return to TocaToca.

## Playback Does Not Start

On smartphones and tablets, audio playback must always be initiated by a direct user action.

Tap the desired recording or the Play button to begin playback.

## The Page Is Not Responding

Reload the page and try again.

If the problem persists, sign in again through [auth.tocatoca.com](https://auth.tocatoca.com) and reopen the player.

## Additional Help

If the problem continues, please contact <a href="mailto:support@tocatoca.com">[support@tocatoca.com](mailto:support@tocatoca.com)</a>.

The following information will help us investigate the issue:

* device type (iPhone, iPad, Android device, etc.)
* browser used
* description of the problem
* details about when the issue occurs

The more information you provide, the easier it will be for us to identify the cause.
