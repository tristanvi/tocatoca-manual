Collections bring together recordings that belong together in a meaningful way. These may be releases by a particular orchestra, thematic compilations, or curated sections of the TocaToca catalog.

## Opening a Collection

Select a collection from the homepage, search results, or another area of the catalog.

![TocaToca Collections Overview](/images/manual/home-001.jpg)

*The homepage displays the available collections. Click on a cover to open the recordings it contains.*

![Opened Collection with Recordings](/images/manual/collection-001.jpg)

*An open collection displays all included recordings together with additional information such as artists, year, genre, and other details.*

## Exploring Recordings Within a Collection

Inside a collection, you can:

* play individual recordings
* play the entire collection
* save recordings as favorites
* add recordings to playlists

Collections are often an excellent starting point for discovering an artist, orchestra, musical style, or historical period.

## Discovering Related Recordings

Many pieces of information within a collection are clickable.

Examples include:

* artists
* orchestras
* singers
* genres
* composers
* lyricists

Clicking any of these links opens a list of all available recordings associated with that item.

This allows you to explore the catalog step by step and discover new musical connections along the way.
