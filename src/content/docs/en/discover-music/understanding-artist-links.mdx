---
title: Understanding Artist Links
description: Why artist links in TocaToca lead directly to related recordings.
---

In TocaToca, artist links are designed to get you to the music as quickly as possible. Instead of opening a technical information page, clicking an artist link immediately displays a list of related recordings.

## What Happens When You Click?

When you click on an artist name, TocaToca displays all available recordings associated with that person or ensemble.

This may include:

* a singer
* an orchestra
* an instrumentalist
* a composer
* a lyricist
* another contributor

![Recording List After Clicking an Artist Link](/images/manual/artist-001.jpg)

*The selected name becomes an active filter. All recordings associated with that artist are displayed.*

![Recording List After Clicking an Orchestra Link](/images/manual/artist-002.jpg)

*Orchestras and other contributors also lead to related recording lists, making it easy to discover additional music from the same artistic circle.*

## Why Is There No Detail Page?

TocaToca was designed for listening to and discovering music.

Instead of showing a technical information page, TocaToca takes you directly to recordings that can be played immediately.

## Ideal for Exploration

Artist links are especially useful when exploring connections within tango history.

With just a few clicks, you can discover:

* additional recordings by the same singer
* other titles performed by an orchestra
* recordings featuring the same musicians
* related artists and ensembles

A single recording often becomes the starting point for an entire musical journey of discovery.
