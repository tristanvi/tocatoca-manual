---
title: Login Problems
description: Help when you are unable to sign in to TocaToca.
---

If you cannot sign in, the cause can usually be identified and resolved quickly.

## Check Your Email Address

Make sure you are using the same email address that was used when creating your TocaToca account.

Pay particular attention to:

* typing mistakes
* accidental spaces
* the correct spelling of the email domain (for example, gmail.com)

## Check Your Password

Make sure your password has been entered correctly.

In particular, check:

* whether Caps Lock is enabled
* whether the correct keyboard layout is selected
* whether any extra spaces have been entered

## Check Your Browser

TocaToca uses cookies to store your login session.

If cookies are disabled or blocked by your browser, sign-in may fail or your session may not be saved.

## Reload the Page

Reload the page and try signing in again.

Sometimes a simple page refresh is enough to resolve a temporary issue.

## Forgotten Password

If you no longer remember your password, use the password reset option on the login page.

## Still Having Problems?

If you are still unable to sign in, please contact <a href="mailto:support@tocatoca.com">[support@tocatoca.com](mailto:support@tocatoca.com)</a>.

Please include:

* the email address you are using
* any error message that is displayed
* the device and browser you are using

The more information you provide, the faster we can help.
