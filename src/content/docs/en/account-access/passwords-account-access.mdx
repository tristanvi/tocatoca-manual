---
title: Passwords and Account Access
description: Information about signing in and managing your password.
---

Access to TocaToca is provided through your email address and personal password.

## Signing In

Open [auth.tocatoca.com](https://auth.tocatoca.com) and sign in using your account credentials.

After a successful login, you will be automatically redirected to the TocaToca player.

## Forgotten Password

If you no longer remember your password, use the **"Forgot Password"** option on the login page.

You will receive an email with instructions on how to reset your password.

## Protecting Your Account

Treat your login credentials as confidential and do not share them with other people.

Each account is intended for personal use. Sharing account credentials may result in restrictions or suspension of access.

For more information, please see [Responsible Use](/en/account-access/responsible-use/).

## Login Problems

If you are unable to sign in, please refer to [Login Problems](/en/account-access/login-problems/) or contact <a href="mailto:support@tocatoca.com">[support@tocatoca.com](mailto:support@tocatoca.com)</a>.
