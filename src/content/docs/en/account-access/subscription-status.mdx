title: Subscription Status

---

Your subscription status shows whether your access to TocaToca is active.

## Check Your Status

Sign in at [auth.tocatoca.com](https://auth.tocatoca.com) and open the **Subscription** section.

![Subscription Status](/images/manual/abonnementstatus.jpg)

## If Your Subscription Is Active

Open [play.tocatoca.com](https://play.tocatoca.com).

With an active subscription, you have access to the complete available catalog.

Alternatively, you can use the **"Open Player"** button to go directly to the TocaToca player.

## If Your Subscription Is Not Active

Follow the instructions in your account area to complete the activation process.

If your access still has not been activated, please contact support at <a href="mailto:support@tocatoca.com">[support@tocatoca.com](mailto:support@tocatoca.com)</a>.
