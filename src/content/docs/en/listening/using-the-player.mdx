---
title: Using the Player
description: Start, pause, and control playback.
---

The player controls the current playback and remains available while you continue browsing the catalog, searching for recordings, or exploring collections.

## Starting and Pausing Playback

Use the Play button to start a recording. Use Pause to stop playback temporarily and continue listening later.

![Desktop Player](/images/manual/player-desktop-001.jpg)

*The desktop player displays the current recording and the most important playback controls.*

On smartphones and tablets, the player is displayed in a layout optimized for smaller screens.

![Mobile Player](/images/manual/player-mobile-001.jpg)

*The mobile player provides the same functionality in a compact format designed for mobile devices.*

## Opening the Player on Mobile Devices

On smartphones and tablets, the player can be opened using the shellac icon.

![Mobile Player Icon](/images/manual/player-mobile-symbol-001.jpg)

The icon can be freely positioned on the screen and remains accessible while you browse the catalog.

## Moving Between Recordings

Use the Previous and Next buttons to move between recordings in the current playlist or queue.

## Adjusting the Volume

Volume can be controlled:

* directly in the player
* through your browser’s volume controls
* using your device’s volume buttons

## Continue Exploring While Listening

You can continue browsing the catalog while playback continues.

Search for artists, open collections, create playlists, or discover new recordings without interrupting the music.

Playback continues as long as your browser remains open and an internet connection is available.
