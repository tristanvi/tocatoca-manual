---
title: Playlists
description: Create and manage your own collections of recordings.
---

Playlists allow you to create personal collections of your favorite recordings and access them whenever you like.

Playlists are useful for:

* favorite recordings
* specific orchestras or singers
* practicas and classes
* musical themes or moods

## Creating a Playlist

Open the **Playlists** section and create a new playlist.

Choose a meaningful name so that you can easily find it again later.

![Playlist with Saved Recordings](/images/manual/playlist-001.jpg)

*A playlist contains your selected recordings and can be expanded or modified at any time.*

## Adding Recordings to a Playlist

Recordings can be added from search results, collections, artist lists, and other areas of the catalog.

Select one or more recordings using the checkbox to the left of each title.

![Selecting Recordings and Adding Them to a Playlist](/images/manual/symbols-header-list.jpg)

After selecting recordings, additional actions become available in the list header.

Choose **"Add to Playlist"** and then select the desired playlist.

## Adding Multiple Recordings at Once

You can select any number of recordings and add them to a playlist in a single step.

This is particularly useful when working with complete search results, collections, or artist lists.

## Playing a Playlist

Open the playlist you would like to listen to and start playback.

You can:

* play the entire playlist
* start individual recordings
* change the order of recordings
* add or remove recordings

## Editing Playlists

Playlists can be modified at any time.

You can add recordings, remove recordings, or create additional playlists for different purposes.

Over time, playlists become your own personal collection of historical recordings.
