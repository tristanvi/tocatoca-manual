TocaToca brings historical recordings directly to your browser.

The initial catalog is based on TangoTunes and features carefully restored transfers from the shellac era. Additional collections and musical genres will be added over time.

With TocaToca, you can discover recordings, explore artists, save favorites, and create your own playlists — all without installing any software and entirely within your browser.

This manual will guide you through your first steps and help you explore the catalog and its unique features.
